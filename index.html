01 / THE DIRECTION

A broader foundation
for medical AI research.

We are building and evaluating experimental components—not presenting a clinically validated product. The longer-term goal is an independent medical AI platform that can support different research and educational applications.

01

Beyond one specialty

An independent model and research framework with multi-specialty medical education as a long-term direction.

02

Evidence before claims

Controlled evaluation, careful data separation, source checking and transparent reporting of limitations.

03

Designed for integration

Exploring clear boundaries between the model, connected applications and future authenticated services.

02 / FIRST INTEGRATION

Meet NeuroSpark.

NeuroSpark is a separate neuroscience-focused research application and the first planned integration for Ceruvi. Together, they represent an exploration of how specialized applications might work with a broader medical AI foundation.

Both projects remain in development. Research demonstrations and synthetic-case testing do not establish clinical usefulness or reliability.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Built with questions.
Not assumptions.

A

Research is not clinical validation

Experimental models require qualified, independent evaluation before anyone can rely on them in healthcare.

B

Privacy and boundaries matter

Development uses controlled research environments. This public website does not collect medical reports, symptoms or patient information.

C

Make limitations visible

We aim to distinguish what has been tested from what remains a research goal.

WHAT'S NEXT

A work in progress.
With purpose.

We're continuing research and evaluation behind the scenes. The website is an introduction to the projects, not access to a model, medical service, or patient portal.
